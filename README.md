# vrinda-store-data-analysis & Excel Dashboard
Excel-based data analytics project analyzing Vrinda Store sales data to uncover sales trends, customer behavior, order patterns, and business performance using an interactive dashboard.

## Dataset Used

## Project Overview
Vrinda Store Data Analysis is a data analytics project created using Microsoft Excel to analyze store sales data and build an interactive business dashboard.
The project involves transforming raw sales data into meaningful information through data cleaning, data processing, analysis, Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, and dashboard visualizations.
The main purpose of this project is to understand sales performance, customer purchasing patterns, order trends, and other important business metrics that can help management make data-driven decisions.

## Questions (KPIs)
- Which month generated the highest sales and orders? 
- How do monthly sales trends compare with order volume? 
- Which gender contributes the most to overall sales? 
- What percentage of orders are successfully delivered? 
- What percentage of orders are returned, cancelled, or refunded? 
- Which are the top 5 states by sales? 
- Which state generates the highest sales? 
- Which age group contributes the most to sales? 
- Which sales channel contributes the highest number of orders? 
- Which customer segment and sales channel should Vrinda Store prioritize for future growth?

## Project Insight
- Total Sales Performance: Vrinda Store generated approximately ₹21.18 million in sales from 28,471 unique orders during 2022.
- Monthly Sales Trend: Sales were strongest in March, reaching approximately ₹1.93 million. Sales then generally declined toward the end of the year, with November recording the lowest monthly sales at approximately ₹1.62 million.
- Gender-wise Sales: Women contributed approximately 64% of total sales, while men contributed around 36%, making women the strongest customer segment.
- Order Fulfillment: Approximately 92.6% of orders were successfully delivered, indicating strong overall order fulfillment. Returned orders accounted for about 3.5%, cancelled orders 2.9%, and refunded orders 1.8%.
- Top Performing States: Maharashtra generated the highest sales at approximately ₹2.99 million, followed by Karnataka (₹2.65M) and Uttar Pradesh (₹2.10M).
- Sales Channels: Amazon generated the highest number of orders with approximately 37.3% of total orders, followed by Myntra (24.9%) and Flipkart (23.0%).
- Age & Gender: The Adult Women segment is the strongest customer segment in the dashboard, contributing the largest share of sales compared with the other age-gender combinations.
- Business Opportunity: The strong contribution from women customers, particularly adults, suggests an opportunity to focus marketing campaigns and product offerings toward this segment.

## Final Conclusion:
The Vrinda Store dashboard provides a clear overview of sales, customers, orders, and business performance. Women customers, Maharashtra, and Amazon are the major contributors to overall performance. The high delivery rate indicates strong order fulfillment, while declining sales toward year-end highlight areas for improvement. Overall, the analysis helps identify key trends and supports data-driven business decision-making.
